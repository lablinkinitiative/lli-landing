U.S. Department of Energy / ORISE
SULI — Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships
SULI places undergraduate students in paid research roles at one of the 17 DOE national laboratories. Students work directly with lab scientists on real research projects in energy, environment, computing, materials science, and more. One of the most prestigious undergraduate research experiences available.

U.S. Department of Energy / ORISE
CCI — Community College Internships
CCI is specifically designed for community college students — one of the few federal internship programs that actively recruits CC students for research at national labs. A direct pathway from community college to a DOE national laboratory internship. LabLink Initiative was founded specifically to help CC students access this program.

Mickey Leland Energy Fellowship
The Mickey Leland Energy Fellowship is a 10-week summer research program for historically underrepresented students in STEM. Fellows work at DOE facilities on fossil energy and carbon management research. One of the few programs with an explicit focus on expanding access for underrepresented communities.

U.S. Environmental Protection Agency
EPA Greater Research Opportunities (GRO)
EPA GRO Fellowship provides both academic (tuition assistance) and internship support for undergrad students pursuing environmental research. Fellows complete a summer internship at an EPA facility and receive funding toward their degree. Strong alignment with sustainability, environmental science, and policy career paths.
📍 EPA Facilities Nationwide
💰 Up to $17,500/year + internship
