About Us

Founded by students who made the journey

Michael and Sean started LabLink Initiative because they experienced the gap firsthand — and knew how to close it. They didn't just talk about access to national labs. They got there.

Michael Sarmiento
Co-Founder & Executive Director

Michael knows exactly what it takes to go from community college to a national lab internship — because he did it. His career spans DOE/Idaho National Laboratory, the California EPA (CARB), and NNSA/Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, with a background in Business Analytics. As PTK President at Sacramento City College, he co-hosted LLI's first major event — a national webinar with INL reaching hundreds of community college students.

DOE / INL NNSA / LLNL CA EPA / CARB Business Analytics PTK President
Sean Florez
Co-Founder & Director of Technology Innovation

Sean is a Ph.D. Materials Scientist who has worked at Idaho National Laboratory, Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL), and within Joint Staff at the Department of Defense as a Foreign Technology Officer. He holds two patents (US-2024-0076793A1 and US-1154-4916B2) and brings deep technical and R&D expertise to LLI's platform and partnerships. He built the AI-powered CE-CDP platform that sits at the core of LLI's student services.

Ph.D. Materials Science (CU Boulder) INL AFRL DoD / Joint Staff 2 Patents

How It Works

From enrollment to offer letter — a clear path

Most students don't know these opportunities exist, or think they're not qualified. We fix both problems.

1

Book a Free Consultation

15 minutes with someone who's done it. No pitch. We learn where you are and where you want to go.

2

Assess Your Skills

Use our skill gap tool to map your background against national lab and industry roles. Know exactly what to build.

3

Build Your Profile

We help you craft a Zintellect profile, personal statement, and resume that speaks to what labs and agencies actually look for.

4

Apply with Confidence

SULI, CCI, NASA, AFRL and industry applications reviewed before submission. Real feedback from people who know the process.

5

Land the Role

Paid placement at a national lab, federal agency, or industry partner. The career pathway starts here.
