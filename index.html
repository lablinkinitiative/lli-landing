What We Offer

Everything You Need to Land a National Lab Internship

From identifying opportunities to submitting a winning application — we support college students at every step. All programs are 100% free.

🔬

National Lab Internships

Paid placements at DOE National Laboratories through programs like SULI (10–16 weeks for undergrads) and CCI (10-week summer for community college students). Also industry, federal agency, and research institution placements.

Browse openings
🎯

Skill Gap Assessment

Our AI-powered CE-CDP platform analyzes your background, identifies gaps, and creates a personalized roadmap to clean energy and STEM careers. Built for students in engineering, data science, business, and sustainability.

Take the assessment
🤝

Mentorship & Coaching

1:1 pairing with professionals at National Labs and federal agencies who went the same path — many from community college. Virtual office hours, application review, and career goal workshops.

How It Works

Five Steps to Your First National Lab Experience

The pathway from college student to National Lab intern is clearer than you think. Here's how we support you at every stage.

1

Discover Programs

Learn about SULI, CCI, and other paid DOE internship programs that may not be on your radar.

2

Assess Your Fit

Use our skill gap tool to identify your strengths and find the best-match internship opportunities.

3

Build Your Application

Work with a mentor to craft your research proposal, resume, and personal statement.

4

Apply & Interview

Submit through Zintellect or lab-specific portals. We coach you through the interview process.

5

Launch Your Career

Gain real research and industry experience, add it to your resume, and open doors that last a lifetime.
